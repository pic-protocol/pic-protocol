Data structures follow the PIC Prover & Verifier and Lineage Guardrail reference profiles (v0.2). A
      PCA carries invariants.operations, a proofOfRelationship binding it to exactly one
      predecessor, a fresh continuation challenge, and one executor proof. Monotonicity: Cₖ ⊆ C₀.

    
Operations are application-defined scope strings, like OAuth scopes — PIC assigns them no universal meaning. The
      resource and audience are carried separately, in the executionContract and the request, not baked
      into the label. Select any executor to explore its associated metadata — its Executor Attestation and its
      Executor DID, the identity and key it signs its PCA with.

    
Composition never merges lineages: a governed parent carries them in multiLineage.carriedLineages under operation
      ENFORCE — PIC of PIC. Each carried lineage keeps its own origin, PoR, and authority context.
