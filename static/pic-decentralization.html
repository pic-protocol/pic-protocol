Subchains and Checkpoints

Authority starts in a single governance box: a wallet or IdP creates the authority token, and that IdP can itself be PIC‑X compliant. PIC‑X exchange lives in the same boundary, then sends a compact checkpoint into a fast subchain across a decentralized constellation: three moving swarms of autonomous AI agents. Governance programs policy into the Trust Anchor guardrails before the agents move: every handoff crosses a governed policy check, and any bypass is rejected. In this example every fourth signed agent hop returns to PIC-X and folds into a compact checkpoint. Governance remains anchored at the IdP source and at PIC‑X enforcement.

What the moving token means

The bright carrier starts inside governance, where IdP authority and PIC‑X exchange are already bound together. The checkpoint then jumps into a moving swarm of autonomous AI agents; each signed hop adds a local transition only after crossing a governance-controlled Trust Anchor policy check. The amber policy rails show Governance programming the guardrails that execute those policies. The links return and fold back into one compact checkpoint before the next group starts.

Governance stays anchored

Wallets and IdPs create authority. PIC-X-compliant IdPs can originate it, while PIC-X propagates and enforces it.

Direct when safe

The hot path is local: autonomous AI agents can move quickly, but every local handoff still crosses a governance-controlled policy check.

Compact after return

PIC-X validates each four-agent history and emits one checkpoint. Receivers after it do not replay every inner agent transition.

This is an explanatory visualization, not a wire-format trace. It sketches authority propagation: a wallet or PIC-X-compliant IdP originates authority, PIC-X materializes a compact PIC Token JWT checkpoint, direct AI-agent hops carry a bounded subchain through Trust Anchors that execute governance policy, and revocation can arrive from the source or from PIC-X policy.
